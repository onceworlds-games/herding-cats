## Textures

From [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com): WoodFloor062, Planks037A, Carpet016, Tiles036, Tiles074, Tiles107, Grass004,
Bricks097, Plaster001, Fabric028. Reduced to grayscale detail maps and tinted in the game.

## Sound

From Kenney (CC0), trimmed and joined into `public/sfx/sprite.mp3`: [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)
(footsteps on wood, carpet, concrete and grass; wood, glass, plate, bell, tin and soft impacts),
[RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) (metal latch and click, leather pouch, cloth, book pages, coins),
[Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) (click, toggle, tick, pluck, glass, maximize),
[Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio) (chips) and [Music Jingles](https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles)
(sax, steel drum and pizzicato jingles). The meows, purrs, claps and the jazz trio are made in code.
